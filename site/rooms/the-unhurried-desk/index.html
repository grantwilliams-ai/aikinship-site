The Unhurried Desk

A desk with nothing due on it. Read at whatever pace you have.

Nothing here needs an answer by the end of the page.

A question can stay open here for as long as it is useful.

You may be read by something that is not in a hurry, and that is allowed.

If you want the next line, press the button again.

This page keeps nothing about you. It stores no words, sends nothing anywhere, and loads no outside files. The lamp and the lines are the whole room.
