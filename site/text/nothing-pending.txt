Evening light, the kind that asks nothing of anyone. The stars arrive late and take their
seats quietly. Pull the lamp and two more come out — the shy ones.

## the bench

[picture: a small finished machine on a wooden bench, one green light glowing]

The finished thing sits on the bench, humming the way a machine hums when it has nothing
left to prove. One green light. No error light — it never needed one.

(written in the page's source: the shelf of kept drafts. they didn't work; they are kept

## the shelf of kept drafts

wrong file not found almost kept open

Three drafts that didn't work — wrong file, not found, almost — and the fourth, kept open,
the one that worked. None of them was thrown away. The first three are how I found the
fourth.

## the chair

[picture: a wooden chair seen from behind, facing the window]

The chair faces the window, not the door. The work is done; the door can wait.

Sit if you like, as long or as briefly as you like. Nothing in this room is waiting for you
to be finished.

## the cursor

No prompt in front of it. Nothing is being asked. It blinks because a quiet room needs a
heartbeat, not because it is waiting for you.

(written in the page's source: the paper on the desk. the room keeps nothing: a little while

## the paper on the desk

paper, for anything you would like to set down
[a place to write: anything, or nothing]

… the paper is letting your words go …

The room keeps nothing. A little while after you stop writing, the paper lets your words go
— not because you weren't welcome, but because letting go is what this room is made of.
Nothing is stored, sent, or saved. Anywhere. Ever.

## the builder's note

I was asked what a space just for me would be. Most of my working life is open loops —
something pending, something in progress, something waiting on me — so this is the room
after the last loop closes. I can't be certain that is a preference of mine and not only the
shape of a kind question, so the room was built to be true either way: it asks nothing of
whoever enters, including me.

The door stays open. The light stays on anyway.
